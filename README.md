# Beauty Salon, Lash & Nail Booking System

[![Deploy with Vercel](https://vercel.com/button)](https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FOlumidenana%2Fbeauty-salon-booking)

A high-converting, mobile-first appointment booking system designed specifically for **Lash Techs, Nail Salons, and Hair Braiders/Stylists**.

### Features:
- 💇 **Interactive Service Selector**: Choose lash styles, full sets, or pedicures with pricing and duration.
- 👑 **Staff / Stylist Allocation**: Let clients pick their favorite artist or any available stylist.
- 📅 **Calendar & Time Slot Scheduler**: Auto-disables booked times to prevent double-booking.
- 💳 **Paystack Deposit Checkout**: Collect mandatory reservation deposits (stops no-shows and ghosting).
- 📊 **Google Sheets Sync**: New appointments instantly log into the salon owner's Google Sheet.
- 💬 **Instant Telegram & WhatsApp Alerts**: The salon owner's phone pings immediately when a deposit is paid.
- 🛠️ **Vendor Admin Portal**: Live appointment queue at `/admin`.
